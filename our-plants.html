Our plants

Plants suited to our island.

We grow and propagate plants suited to the conditions of Mauritius, with a focus on native and endemic species that form part of the island’s botanical heritage.

Our plants become available at different times throughout the year. To find out what is currently available, or to enquire about a particular species, please contact us directly.
