Native & Endemic Flora

Our nursery focuses on propagating Mauritius’ native and endemic plants, species that are naturally adapted to the island’s climate, environment and growing conditions.

Propagating these plants locally contributes to their conservation and helps maintain living populations of Mauritius’ native and endemic flora. It also supports the continued cultivation and appreciation of species that are an important part of the island’s natural environment.

From time to time, plants from our propagation work may become available when quantities allow. Availability depends on the species being propagated and the plants that are ready for distribution. If you would like to enquire about plants currently available, contact us .
