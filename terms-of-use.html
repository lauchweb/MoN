About this website

This website provides information about Mo Endemik Nursery, its work in the conservation and propagation of Mauritius’ native and endemic flora, and plants that may be made available periodically.

Plant availability

Plants are made available periodically and availability may change without notice. Information on this website does not guarantee that a particular plant will be available at any given time.

Plant information

Information about plants is provided for general guidance. Growing conditions, establishment and performance may vary depending on location, care, soil and other environmental conditions.

Enquiries

If you would like to know whether plants are currently available, please contact the nursery directly at plant@moendemik.mu .

Website content

We aim to keep the information on this website accurate and useful, but content may be updated, changed or removed as our nursery activities develop.

Contact

For questions concerning the nursery, plants or the information provided on this website, please contact Mo Endemik through the appropriate contact details provided on the site.
