Privacy Policy

About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Mo Endemik handles personal information that may be provided through this website. We aim to collect and use information only where it is necessary and in a clear and responsible manner.

Information we may collect

Depending on how you use the website, we may receive information that you choose to provide, such as your name, email address or other contact details when you get in touch with us or otherwise provide information through the website.

How information is used

Personal information is used only for the purposes for which it is provided, such as responding to enquiries, communicating with supporters, or administering activities connected with Mo Endemik. We do not use personal information for unrelated purposes without an appropriate basis for doing so.

Donations and third-party services

If you make a donation through a third-party payment service, your payment information may be processed directly by that service according to its own privacy policy and terms. Mo Endemik does not need to receive or retain your full payment details in order to receive a donation.

Website services and links

This website may use external services or contain links to other websites. Those services and websites may have their own privacy policies and practices. Mo Endemik is not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites.

Retention and protection

Personal information is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected or where there is another legitimate reason to retain it. Appropriate measures are taken to help protect personal information from loss, misuse or unauthorised access.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including the right to request access to information held about you and to ask for inaccurate information to be corrected. Requests or questions about personal information can be directed to us using the contact details provided on this website.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in the website, our activities or applicable requirements. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
